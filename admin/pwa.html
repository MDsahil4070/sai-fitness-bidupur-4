Deployment Checklist
Before publishing the app
manifest.json

Keep the generated manifest.json in the main GYM-PRO folder.

sw.js

Keep the service worker in the correct scope. The current main app expects sw.js in the GYM-PRO root.

PWA Icons

Keep icon-192.png and icon-512.png available at the paths referenced by the manifest.

HTTPS

Production PWA installation should be served through HTTPS.
